Reported photos

When a moderator reports a photo, the photo is deleted and a record of who sent it is kept here for 90 days, then deleted automatically. Only lead moderators can see this page.

The name, email and phone are what the sender typed, so they may be false. The internet address and provider point to a connection (a home broadband line or phone network), not to a person, and the location is only approximate. Only the police can ask an internet provider who was using a connection. If a photo might be illegal, don't try to trace the person yourself. Give these details to the police (101, or 999 in an emergency). For a sexual image of a child, report it to the police or to CEOP at ceop.police.uk.
