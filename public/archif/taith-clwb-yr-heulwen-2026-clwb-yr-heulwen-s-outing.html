Taith Clwb yr Heulwen 2026 Clwb yr Heulwen's outing

17eg o Fedi oedd dyddiad y daith eleni a terfyn y siwrne oedd i'r Ganolfan Haearn a Dur ger Croesoswallt. Roedd 43 wedi teithio, rhai yn aelodau o Glwb yr Heulwen ac eraill yn ffrindiau a theulu. Ar y ffordd adref, cafwyd cyfle i aros yn Llangollen am gyfnod byr. Cafwyd diwrnod gwych efo llawer o gymdeithasu. Diolch i haeloni Fferm Wynt Brenig am ddarparu arian i dalu costau'r bws. September the 17th was the date of the trip this year and the journey took us to the Iron and Steel Centre near Oswestry. 43 came, most being members of Clwb yr Heulwen and others being friends and family. On the journey back we stopped for a short period at Llangollen. We all had a wonderful day. Our thanks go to the Brenig Wind Farm for their generous contribution in paying for the coach.
