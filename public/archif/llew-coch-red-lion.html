LLEW COCH / RED LION

Croeso i Sara i Lansannan. Mae wedi cymeryd y Llew Coch drosodd o ofal Lee ers yr ail wythnos o Fedi 2024. Agorodd am y tro cyntaf ar nos Wener y 13eg o Fedi a cafodd gefnogaeth dda gan drigolion lleol. Dymunwn yn dda iddi yn ei dyletswyddau newydd yma yn y pentref.
Welcome to Llansannan Sara. She has taken the Red Lion over following Lee's departure during the second week of September 2024. First opening day was Friday the 13th and the locals were out to support her.
We wish her well in her new responsibilities here in the village.
Llun trwy ganiatad Mr Alwyn Williams. / Photo by courtesy of Mr Alwyn Williams.
