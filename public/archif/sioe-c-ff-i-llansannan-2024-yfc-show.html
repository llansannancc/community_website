Sioe C Ff I Llansannan 2024 YFC Show

Bu angen defnyddio pebyll ar y cae a'r festri Capel Coffa Henry Rees gan nad oedd y Ganolfan ar gael oherwydd adnewyddu y system gwresogi ar y safle tros y gwyliau haf. Due to the Ganolfan not being available due to the installation of a new heating system over the summer holiday, marquees, on the field, and the Capel Coffa Henry Rees vestry were used.
