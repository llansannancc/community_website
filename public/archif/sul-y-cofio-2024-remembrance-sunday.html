SUL Y COFIO 2024 REMEMBRANCE SUNDAY

Y rhai a gymerodd ran yn y gwasanaeth oedd Mr Trefor Roberts, Miss Tammy Owen, Mr Huw Rawson a Mr Eifion Jones. Those taking roles in the service were Mr Trefor Roberts, Miss Tammy Owen, Mr Huw Rawson and Mr Eifion Jones

Cynhaliwyd eleni ar Ddydd Sul y 10fed o Tachwedd.
Fel yn arferol erbyn hyn roedd cefnogaeth dda o'r gymuned wedi dod i gofio'r rhai a gollwyd yn y ddwy Ryfel Byd.
The date this year was the 10th November. As is usual by now, the support from the community to remember the fallen in the two World Wars was excellent.

Yn ymgasglu cyn dchrau'r gwasanaeth
Gathering near the Memorial prior to the service.

Roedd cynrychiolaeth o Sgowtiaid Crosby yma eto eleni gyda nifer or 12fed Scowtiaid Crosby yn bresnnol.
Crosby Scouts were here again this year with members of the 12th Crosby Scouts being present.

Gosodwyd rhithoedd ar ran Eglwys Sant Sannan gan Miss Tammy Owen ac ar ran y Scowtiaid gan Mr Simon Lawrence a gan Mr Emrys Williams ar ran y Cyngor Cymuned.
Wreaths were laid by Miss Tammy Owen on behalf of St Sannan's Church, Mr Simon Lawrence for Crosby DScouts and Mr Emrys Williams for the Community Council

Yn barod i rwystro trafnudiaeth yn ystod y Gwasanaeth-Mr Jack Howatson ger y Saracens Head.
Ready to stop traffic during the Service-Mr Jack Howatson by the Saracens Head.
